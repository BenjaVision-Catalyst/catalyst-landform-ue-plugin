# Watershed Character

Schema 22 adds an opt-in watershed layer over the existing composed native
procedural surface. The one-knob progression moves from lightly conditioned
major reaches to a more dissected network. It changes channel-area threshold,
channel-bed offset and maximum valley influence together. Rock erodibility scales
the nodal offset; body size, geology, reference rainfall, runoff fraction, declared
internal terminals and analysis budgets remain independently authored.

This is a bounded static drainage/terrain-conditioning profile. It is not a
hydraulic, groundwater, climate or mass-conserving erosion/sediment simulator.
Natural pre-excavation catchments remain immutable during native player sculpting.
A mine breach or dam is not yet reflected in live drainage/water state. Consumers
must not use this natural graph as proof of edited-world hydraulic connectivity.

## Native reconciliation

The retained October watershed research supplied the canonical octasphere,
Priority-Flood, area accounting and shared channel-reference construction. Its
private analytic terrain and detached diagnostic shape adapter were not brought
forward. Current analysis samples the actual native VPP-backed composed planet,
after macro structures/regions and optional elevation distribution, before volume
excavation and live sculpting. The existing planet stamp remains the sole binding.

Installed VPP 2.0p8 source was searched for watershed, drainage, hydrology and
erosion generation. No finite spherical catchment/routing producer was found.
Native layers, stamps, noise, materials, LOD, moving collision and sculpt saves
continue to own voxel execution. UE's Landscape erosion tools edit Landscape
heightmaps; they do not supply this spherical VPP recipe analysis. Native water
rendering is not substituted for watershed topology.

Native height and volume spline stamps were also inspected. Height-spline nearest
keys use planar XY; `GetHeightAlongSpline` returns spline Z. Volume splines support
full 3D closest keys, transforms and graph-defined distances and remain available
for authored spline geometry. They do not supply connected catchment accounting,
lake/ocean policy or this shared triangulated planetary bed reference. This
profile does not add spline interpolation or nearest-spline code: its specialization
is a spherical network/reference field, emitted through the existing planet stamp.
Native spline graphs are programmable; no claim is made that VPP cannot express
an alternative graph-based channel model.

UE GeometryCore's `FDynamicMesh3` and `FDynamicMeshAABBTree3` index the immutable
analysis surface. Its native watertight ray query avoids shared-edge sampling gaps
exposed by the channel tests. Native UE array heaps provide routing queues. This
mesh is analysis data, never a terrain presenter. There is no custom ray caster,
mesher, streamer, terrain LOD scheduler or sculpt serializer.

[Priority-Flood](https://arxiv.org/abs/1511.04463) supplies the depression/spill
analysis basis and supports irregular meshes. Filling a potential does not raise
the terrain or imply that a lake is currently full. The earlier prepared native
prototype had no executed VPP qualification; current checks run the current code.

## Surface order and versioning

1. Compile existing native terrain, continents/plates, broad features and regions.
2. Apply existing SurfaceMin/Max fitting and optional area/elevation distribution.
3. Analyse that surface and condition selected channel corridors.
4. Analyse the conditioned surface again for final catchments and reference runoff.
5. Compose existing volume operations, native sculpting and materials afterward.

An explicit OceanHeight guide is required. Measured schema-19 resources remain
source-pinned and refuse watershed authoring. Inactive schema-22 state preserves
the corresponding schema-21 field exactly. Loading schemas 1–21 does not enable
or reinterpret watersheds. The strict portable codec admits schema 22; older
binaries reject it. Upstream elevation/rock edits retain schema 22 and rebuild
dependent drainage. Local Landform editing retains its disposable local context.

## Routing, ocean policy and budgets

A canonical, uniformly subdivided octasphere samples body-space unit directions.
Each spherical triangle contributes a third of its exact nominal spherical area
to its vertices. Areas sum to `4*pi*referenceRadius²`; rugged surface area is not
claimed. The analysis is independent of VPP chunks and presentation LOD.

The largest connected component below OceanHeight is this profile's reference
ocean. Other disconnected sub-sea depressions remain inland basins. This explicit
single-ocean policy is not a universal classification of every isolated sea.
Additional declared closed terminals are snapped to analysis cells. Aliased
terminals or a closed terminal in the reference ocean refuse atomically. All-land
worlds select a lowest-cell internal terminal; all-ocean worlds have no invented
rivers. Closed terminals receive reference inflow, not ocean export.

Priority-Flood assigns spill potential and discovery parents. Descending neighbours
use greatest spill drop per angular distance; deterministic cell order resolves
ties/flats. Every receiver has lower flood rank. Reverse traversal accumulates
nominal contributing area and runoff. Reference rainfall is metres per 365.25-day
year; only the authored runoff fraction on non-ocean cells enters the graph.
Ocean export plus internal-terminal inflow closes that reference input. Storage,
evaporation, infiltration, groundwater and current lake stage are not solved.

The default uses seven subdivisions: 65,538 cells and 131,072 faces. Lower requested
levels may refine within the accepted budget to resolve physical influence widths.
A width below the resulting maximum edge, insufficient cell budget or unsupported
input refuses without coarsening, rerolling seeds or dropping topology. The maximum
is 65,538 cells; this does not qualify global metre-scale drainage on Earth-sized
worlds. Resolution sensitivity and individual failures remain explicit.

## Channel construction and final drainage

Original routing selects reaches above an absolute contributing-area threshold.
Ocean, potential-lake and immediate lake-shore cells are excluded from constructed
reaches. A shared piecewise-linear reference on the native analysis mesh assigns
node bed heights with a minimum downstream grade. Every incident reach uses that
same reference, so junctions agree and accepted centerlines remain graded.

A bounded graph-distance influence blends the existing native terrain toward this
reference. Main valleys receive greater influence widths than tributaries, based
on contributing area, with a resolution floor. The advanced width is the largest
influence scale, not a guaranteed excavated cross-section width. Nodal incision is
not the maximum pointwise cut: interpolation can condition small humps or fill
local lows. Measured cut/fill is recorded; no sediment-volume closure is claimed.
Original potential-lake floors remain unchanged below their spill level, with a continuous
shore transition. This constructs no channel across a lake floor.

The final catchments are computed on the conditioned field. Some original
conditioning corridors can become abandoned/captured. Only constructed reaches
that remain final receivers are reported as active graded channels. Other final
routing edges are finite-resolution flow relations, not continuously graded
constructed rivers. There is one conditioning phase and one final analysis, not
an iterative geological evolution or converged continuum hydrology solve.

## Review and qualification

In Planet Designer choose **Geology → Watershed**. The native main row contains its
eye toggle, Character slider and disabled reseed icon. Its category chevron opens
the detailed property rows directly below; there is no separate Advanced button.
Watershed-local seeded variation is the next separate task. Character dragging is coalesced and has one
Undo. In **Preview**, **Focus largest channel** points the existing globe camera at a generated
reach and marks it for the existing **Walk here** host. The Preview drainage overlay can be hidden:
blue marks active graded reaches, grey final routing and cyan potential lake rims.
Overlays wait for native terrain publication and are diagnostics, not water meshes.
Watershed previews retain native VPP final quality 4 and draft quality 1. The
historical quality-8 trial exceeded an inspection budget, not a native VPP
presentation limit. Dense inspection is now explicit/lazy; it does not cap
ordinary preview generation. See the [scope and preview review](WatershedScopeReview.md)
for delivered terrain versus diagnostics/water, controls needing scope review
and current presentation gaps.

The library includes a basin study, current continental/rock integration and a
miniature excavation witness. The [qualification record](../conformance/Watershed/qualification-mac-arm64.json)
separates native graph/field checks, finite-resolution limits, rendered cases,
interactive native walking, cooked mining/fill and separate-process sculpt reload.
Native Rock properties and stratum identities remain canonical through excavation.

Next gaps include spatial/reference-data calibration, adaptive global drainage at
larger scales, sediment and hillslope processes, current water/lake state and edit
invalidation. Karst needs explicit soluble-rock, permeability and fracture inputs;
these are not inferred from mining hardness. Multiplayer remains out of scope.
